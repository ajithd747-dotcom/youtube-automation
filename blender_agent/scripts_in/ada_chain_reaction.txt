Meet Ada, a curious little robot with one big idea.

Every morning she walks through her workshop, dreaming up the perfect chain reaction.

First, she polishes her treasures: glass, gold, and a crystal that glows.

Then comes the classic trick. One small push, and a winding line of dominoes topples, one after another.

The last one launches a heavy steel ball, and a whole wall of crates comes crashing down.

A gust of wind unfurls her banner, proud and rippling under the open sky.

At night, fireworks paint the darkness and light up the lake.

Even the jelly joins the party, wobbling and bouncing with pure energy.

Physics is the art of making motion beautiful. Ada calls it her favorite kind of magic.
