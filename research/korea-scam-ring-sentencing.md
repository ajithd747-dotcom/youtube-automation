# Topic: South Korea voice-phishing scam ring sentencing (2026)

## Key facts
- Daejeon District Court's Hongseong branch sentenced 46 people in April 2026 for involvement in a voice-phishing (fraud) ring.
- The ring operated out of Cambodia and Thailand, targeting South Korean victims.
- Ring leader went by the alias "Bugeon" -- a Chinese national; real identity not publicly disclosed in reporting.
- From June 2024 to July 2025, the group defrauded roughly 110 victims of about 9.4 billion Korean won (~$6.8M USD) through voice phishing scams.
- The 46 defendants were charged with fraud, concealment of criminal proceeds, and participating in a criminal organization.
- Sentences ranged from 1 year 8 months to 10 years in prison.
- Separately (related context, not same case): South Korean authorities have been repatriating and arresting nationals returning from Cambodian scam centers, some of whom claim they were coerced/forced to work there.
