# Topic: Best free AI productivity tools 2026

## Key facts
- ChatGPT: versatile — writing, brainstorming, research, summarization, coding, planning.
- Claude, Gemini, Grok, Deepseek, Perplexity: also popular free-tier AI assistants in 2026.
- Zapier AI: describe an automation in plain language, it builds the workflow for you.
- NotebookLM: upload PDFs/docs/links, get summaries and Q&A grounded in your own sources.
- Fathom: AI meeting notetaker/summarizer.
- Perplexity: search engine that reads sources and answers with citations instead of just links.
- Goblin Tools: breaks down projects into subtasks (great for ADHD/overwhelm angle).
- Most tools: free tier available, paid tiers unlock more usage/features.
