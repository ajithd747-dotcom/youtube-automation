# Topic: Comedy premise -- productivity Googling backfires

## Premise
Stickman Googles "how to be productive" -- the first result is "how to procrastinate," and he immediately gets sucked into scrolling instead of doing anything.

## Beats
1. Setup: stickman sits down determined to be productive, opens a search.
2. Escalation: he searches "how to be productive," sees a tempting/distracting result instead.
3. Punchline: he's now scrolling/procrastinating, completely derailed from his original goal -- ironic, relatable payoff.

## Tone
Playful, self-aware, universally relatable (everyone's done this). Quick pacing, no explaining the joke.
