# Topic: Elderly Canadian couple reunited after 8 months apart

## Key facts
- Wolfram Gottschalk (83) and Anita Gottschalk (81), a Canadian couple, were forced to live in separate nursing homes for more than eight months.
- In late August, they were photographed crying during a visitation -- the photo went viral.
- Their granddaughter Ashley Bartyik shared the photos/video that generated significant public attention and support.
- On Thursday, September 22 [2026], Wolfram was moved into the same care facility as Anita.
- They have been married for 62 years.
- Reunion photos/video show them smiling, kissing, and embracing, described as "happy tears" after their long separation.

## Angle
- Warm, romantic, real human-interest story -- 62 years of marriage, forced apart by circumstance, reunited through public support.
